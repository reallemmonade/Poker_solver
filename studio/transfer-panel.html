Take your study data with you

iCloud Drive & Files

Save a study backup to iCloud Drive on your laptop, then open it here on your iPhone. Your backup includes saved solver results and their hands, ranges and bet sizes, strategy models, hand histories, assessments and settings.

Transfers use a file you save and open yourself. There is no automatic iCloud sign-in or background sync. A completed solver result is included; the temporary betting tree and an unfinished calculation are not stored.

Save from this device

Prepare the backup first. Then share it to Files and choose iCloud Drive, or download it and move the file to iCloud Drive. A download alone does not confirm an iCloud copy.

The study file is smaller for your phone. Saved-spot drills do not need the larger strategy models.

Open on another device

Choose your backup from Files → iCloud Drive. You can also open an exported spot-analysis JSON. Import merges records; it does not delete this device's data. Existing device settings stay in place. After importing, use Saved spots to practice.

A backup is a snapshot. After studying on either device, export a new backup to transfer that progress. Browser data can be removed by clearing website data; keep a copy in Files.
