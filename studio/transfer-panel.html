Backup & import

Save a backup file on one device and open it on another. It includes saved solver results and their hands, ranges and bet sizes, strategy models, hand histories, assessments and settings.

Transfers use a file you save and open yourself. Signed out, nothing syncs on its own. A completed solver result is included; the temporary betting tree and an unfinished calculation are not stored.

Save from this device

Prepare the backup, then share or download it to any cloud drive or folder.

The study file is smaller for your phone. Saved-spot drills do not need the larger strategy models.

Open on another device

Choose your backup file. You can also open an exported spot-analysis JSON. Import merges records; it does not delete this device's data. Existing device settings stay in place. After importing, use Saved spots to practice.

A backup is a snapshot. After studying on either device, export a new backup to transfer that progress. Browser data can be removed by clearing website data; keep a copy of the file.
