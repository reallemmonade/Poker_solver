Solving a position

Analyze current spot reconstructs the exact public history and solves a continuation game from that decision. It accepts earlier custom sizes even when absent from a stored blueprint. Only your private cards, public cards, stacks, payouts and public action history are sent to the worker.

Two-player and multiplayer calculations

Two remaining NLH contestants can use the vector CFR+ engine even when the hand began with up to nine players. The original seat order, dead money, side pots and ICM field remain intact. The joint-deal engine also accepts multiplayer pots and the supported community-card variants, within memory and tree limits. It enumerates compatible private deals and future cards, and measures each player's independent best-response gain. Broad multiplayer ranges and preflop trees usually exceed local capacity; accepting those inputs does not mean their full games have been solved. The result reports total deviation gain and mixing regret for your hand. Both must reach the target before recommendations or measured grades are displayed. The target applies to the entered ranges and allowed bet sizes, not unknown real-world ranges or omitted sizes. ICM and rake calculations preserve their conditional utility model; convergence is measured rather than presumed.

Computation limits and imported preflop sources

Heads-up NLH flop and turn studies enumerate all future public cards, compatible private hands and terminal outcomes. Resource limits can require narrower ranges or smaller action trees; unsupported calculations return no recommendation. Imported preflop sources apply only to the matching initial game and complete betting line. Their source percentages are shown separately from measured EV grades. No full-game six- or nine-handed strategy is bundled. The multiplayer engine is a new bounded implementation and has not yet received independent behavioral validation.

Ranges and hidden information

All players retain ranges, including you. Opponent strategies cannot condition on your actual private cards. You may enter weighted current-position ranges. Automatic ranges continue a preceding exact strategy when available; otherwise they explicitly assume all legal hands. Sampled models and opponent personas do not supply inference for production solves. Specified folded ranges retain card bunching; unknown folded hands are treated as uniform and marginalized. Every skipped inference action is counted. Actual past bets remain in the public game state. Exact postflop evaluation enumerates compatible private hands and every future public card.

Results and practice

Analyze displays frequencies, action EV and estimated alternative-action costs and saves the exact request for reuse. A changed range, tree or requested budget triggers a new solve. Quiz prepares the solution before each decision and hides its answer until the hand ends. Prepared results are reused. Whole-hand EV totals require measured costs for every choice; source-only references do not invent missing values.

Rules and utility

The integer-chip engine supports NLH, PLO4/5/6, Omaha Hi/Lo and configurable community-card games, two to nine seats within deck limits, antes, minimum raises, cumulative reopening, side pots, returned uncalled chips, odd chips and capped rake. Tournament ICM supports twelve remaining stacks; bounty utility includes immediate cash and omits future retained-bounty value. Future tournament play is not solved. Stud, draw, Razz and Badugi require different game engines.

Implementation and checks

The included Node engine can run the same spot calculations without a browser. See the solver documentation. Engine checks compare exact values, blockers, rake, ICM, independent river calculations and hidden-card isolation. Browser checks cover the delivered standalone file, range changes, persistence, cancellation and full-hand quiz review. These checks establish implementation behavior, not a full-poker GTO certificate.

Algorithm references: OpenSpiel outcome-sampling MCCFR, MCCFR research, and safe subgame-solving research. This local continuation engine does not implement the safe-resolving boundary gadgets described in that last paper.
